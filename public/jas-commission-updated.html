How a commission flows

You get an invoice first, and the work starts once your downpayment is confirmed.

  1. Send your request

    Message us on Facebook or by email with what you need and your deadline.

  2. Receive your invoice

    We send an official invoice with the price before the task begins.

  3. Pay the 50% downpayment

    This confirms your order. Orders are only processed after the downpayment is confirmed.

  4. We start your task

    We may send a sample or progress update to show your work has begun.

  5. Settle the balance

    Pay the remaining balance once the task is complete.

  6. Receive your final file

    The final file is sent after the balance is paid in full.

Why we require a downpayment first

  1. A downpayment confirms your order and allows us to begin your commission.
  2. We may send a sample or progress update to show that your work has already started.
  3. Some clients stop replying after receiving the sample, leaving the time and work unpaid.
  4. To protect our time and keep the process fair, we do not provide the full service before payment confirmation.
